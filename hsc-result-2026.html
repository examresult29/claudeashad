How to Check HSC Result 2026 Online

  1. Go to educationboardresults.gov.bd.
  2. Examination: select HSC/Alim/Equivalent.
  3. Year: 2026, then select your Board.
  4. Enter your Roll and Registration number from your admit card.
  5. Solve the sum (captcha) and click Submit.

If the server is busy: the site is slow for the first 1–2 hours after release. Don't keep refreshing — wait a few minutes and try again, or use the SMS method below, which is usually the fastest.

HSC Result 2026 by SMS (16222)

Send an SMS in the format below from any mobile operator. The reply gives your GPA and subject-wise grades; for the full marksheet, use the online method.

HSC <space> first 3 letters of board <space> Roll <space> 2026 → 16222

Build your SMS

Your SMS: HSC DHA 123456 2026

Send to 16222

If you send the SMS before the result is out (pre-registration), you get the reply as soon as the result is published. SMS charge is about Tk 2.44, depending on the operator.

HSC Result 2026 with Marksheet

To see subject-wise marks and grades, use eboardresults.com:

  1. Exam: HSC/Alim/Equivalent, Year: 2026, then select your board.
  2. Result Type: Individual Result.
  3. Enter your roll and registration number, complete the captcha and click Get Result.
  4. Use Print → Save as PDF to keep a copy — you will need it for admission applications.

Full results for a college: on the same site choose Institution Result and enter the EIIN number. Centre, upazila and district-wise results are also available there.

HSC GPA Calculator and Grading System

MarksGradeGrade point
80–100A+5.00
70–79A4.00
60–69A-3.50

40–49C2.00
33–39D1.00
0–32F0.00

How it works: HSC has 6 compulsory/group subjects (Bangla, English, ICT and 3 subjects from your group) plus 1 fourth (optional) subject. From the fourth subject's grade point, 2 is subtracted and the rest is added to your total, which is then divided by 6. GPA is capped at 5.00. An F in any compulsory subject gives GPA 0.00 (fail). You must also pass the creative (CQ), MCQ and practical parts separately.

HSC Re-scrutiny 2026 (Board Challenge)

If your result is lower than expected, you can apply for re-scrutiny for about a week starting the day after the result. Answer scripts are not re-marked from scratch — the board checks for errors in adding marks, unmarked answers, OMR mistakes and similar issues.

  • Fee: Tk 150 per paper (for two-paper subjects, both papers must be selected).
  • By SMS (Teletalk): RSC DHA Roll SubjectCode → 16222 (for several subjects, separate codes with commas, e.g. RSC DHA 123456 101,107). You will receive a PIN; then send RSC YES PIN ContactNumber → 16222.
  • Online: through the board's re-scrutiny portal, paying by bKash, Nagad or Teletalk.

The 2026 application dates and process will be confirmed here once the boards publish their notice.

HSC Result 2026: FAQ

When will the HSC Result 2026 be published?

The boards have proposed any day between 25 and 29 October 2026. The final date will be announced after ministry approval.

How do I check the HSC result by SMS?

Send HSC DHA 123456 2026 to 16222. For Alim: ALIM MAD 123456 2026.

How do I get my HSC result with marksheet?

On eboardresults.com, choose Individual Result and enter your roll and registration numbers.

What is the pass mark in HSC?

At least 33% in every subject. An F in any compulsory subject means fail; an F in the fourth subject does not.

What was the HSC pass rate in 2025?

58.83%, with 69,097 GPA-5 (published 16 October 2025).
