What is HSC Result?

The HSC Result 2026 refers to the board result of the Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) examination in Bangladesh. HSC stands for Higher Secondary Certificate, the qualification for completing Class 12 in Bangladesh’s education system. This year, 1,270,583 students appeared for the HSC and equivalent exams across 11 education boards, from 9,439 educational institutions. The written exams began on July 2, 2026, and ended on August 8, 2026, with practical tests completed by August 15, 2026. Some papers under the Chattogram board were postponed due to flooding and were completed on September 9, 2026.

Most subjects feature a 100-mark structure, split into a 25-mark Multiple Choice Question (MCQ) section and a 75-mark Creative Question (CQ) section (subjects with practicals are 25 MCQ + 50 CQ + 25 practical). English is a single written exam without MCQ. You must pass each part (CQ, MCQ and practical) separately.

HSC Result 2026

The 11 education boards, including nine general boards (Dhaka, Rajshahi, Cumilla, Jashore, Chattogram, Barishal, Sylhet, Dinajpur, and Mymensingh), the Madrasah Education Board, and the Technical Education Board, conduct the Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) Exam.

To pass the HSC, you need at least 33 marks in each subject. Failing any compulsory subject means you fail the entire exam, no matter your GPA in other subjects. Failing only the fourth (optional) subject does not make you fail. The results are published by all 11 education boards: nine general boards, one Madrasah board for Alim exams, and one Technical board for vocational streams.

HSC Result 2026 Date and Time

You might wonder when your HSC Result 2026 will come out. The results are usually published within 60 days after the exams end. The written exams started on July 2, 2026, and ended on August 8, 2026. However, some Chattogram board papers were postponed because of flooding and were held until September 9, 2026, so the result has moved to late October.

On October 12, 2026, the Inter-Education Board Coordination Committee said the boards have proposed publishing the HSC Result 2026 on any day between October 25 and 29, 2026. The exact date will be fixed after the Ministry of Education approves it.

The result is usually handed over to the head of government in the morning (around 10:00 AM), followed by a press conference. After that, students can check results online and by SMS. Official updates appear on the Ministry of Education or education board websites.

Beware of websites showing fixed dates such as October 7, October 8 or October 16 — no exact date has been officially announced yet.

How to check HSC result 2026

Once the HSC Result 2026 is published, you can access your result through several methods: online and SMS. Online checking gives full details, including your HSC Result Marksheet, and works best with a stable internet connection. SMS provides quick GPA information without needing the internet.

HSC Result by SMS

The SMS method offers a quick and reliable way to check your HSC Result, especially when internet access is limited or websites are overloaded on result day. This method gives you your GPA and subject-wise grades instantly but doesn’t include the full HSC Result Marksheet. To check your result via SMS, follow these steps:

To check your result via SMS, follow these steps:

  1. Open your mobile phone’s messaging app.
  2. Compose a message in this format: HSC <space> Board Code <space> Roll Number <space> 2026
  3. Example: For a Dhaka Board student with roll number 123456, type: HSC DHA 123456 2026. For Alim (Madrasah) students, type: ALIM MAD 123456 2026.
  4. Send the message to 16222 from any mobile operator in Bangladesh (charge about Tk 2.44).

Tip: if you send the SMS before the result is published, it works as pre-registration and you will get your result automatically as soon as it is released.

Education Board Result

The HSC Exam is managed by 11 education boards in Bangladesh. These boards oversee specific regions or educational streams: nine general boards (Dhaka, Chattogram, Rajshahi, Cumilla, Jashore, Sylhet, Barishal, Dinajpur, and Mymensingh), the Bangladesh Madrasah Education Board (Alim), and the Bangladesh Technical Education Board (HSC BM/Vocational). The HSC Result With Marksheet is important because it provides a detailed breakdown of student performance beyond just the GPA. The marksheet shows the grade and grade point for each subject along with your cumulative GPA (with and without the fourth subject). GPA Calculation

The Education Boards of Bangladesh use a standardized grading system to assign Grade Point Averages (GPAs) based on subject-wise marks. The minimum passing mark per subject is 33, and failing any compulsory subject means you fail overall. The grading scale for HSC 2026 is:

GradeMarks RangeGPA
A+80–1005.00
A70–794.00
A-60–693.50
B50–593.00
C40–492.00
D33–391.00
F0–320.00

To calculate your GPA:

  1. Convert each subject’s marks (including theory, practical, and MCQ) to a grade point using the above scale. Subjects with two papers (e.g., Bangla 1st & 2nd) are combined into one subject.
  2. Add the grade points of your 6 compulsory/group subjects (Bangla, English, ICT and 3 group subjects).
  3. For the fourth (optional) subject, subtract 2 from its grade point and add only the remainder (if any).
  4. Divide the total by 6. The maximum GPA is 5.00.

Example Calculation:

  • Suppose a student gets: Bangla (A+, 5.00), English (A, 4.00), ICT (A-, 3.50), Physics (B, 3.00), Chemistry (A+, 5.00), Higher Math (A, 4.00) and fourth subject Biology (A+, 5.00).
  • Total of 6 subjects: 5.00 + 4.00 + 3.50 + 3.00 + 5.00 + 4.00 = 24.50
  • Fourth subject bonus: 5.00 – 2 = 3.00 → total 27.50
  • GPA = 27.50 / 6 = 4.58

HSC Re-scrutiny Application

If your HSC Result 2026 doesn’t meet your expectations, you can apply for re-scrutiny. Re-scrutiny, also called a Board Challenge, is a process where the education boards recheck your answer scripts for possible errors — for example, mistakes in adding marks, unmarked answers, or OMR (MCQ) sheet errors. You can challenge specific subject results.

Any student who receives their HSC results can apply for re-scrutiny in one or more subjects. Applications usually open the day after the result and stay open for about a week.

  • Fee: Tk 150 per paper (for two-paper subjects, both papers must be selected).
  • By SMS (Teletalk only): type RSC <space> Board <space> Roll <space> Subject Code and send to 16222. For several subjects, separate codes with commas, e.g. RSC DHA 123456 101,107. You will receive a PIN; then send RSC YES PIN Contact-Number to 16222.
  • Online: through the education board’s re-scrutiny portal, with payment by bKash, Nagad or Teletalk.

The main benefit of a Board Challenge is the chance to improve your marks and GPA, which could help with university admissions or scholarships. The exact 2026 application dates will be added here once the boards publish their notice.
